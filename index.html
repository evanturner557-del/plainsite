FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, a few photos of your work, and any reviews you're happy to show. We'll write the words with you.

Do you do online shops or booking systems?

Not in these packages. We can link to a booking tool you already use.

Who owns the website?

You do — the files, the domain and the content. There's no lock-in.

Where will it be hosted?

Anywhere you like. Static sites can run on free or very cheap hosting, and we'll help you set it up in your name.

Will I rank on Google?

We build it properly — fast, mobile-friendly, clear titles — but nobody can honestly promise rankings.

When can I book?

We’re opening the first build slots soon. Booking details will appear on this page at launch. No payment is taken today.
