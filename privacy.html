Privacy notice

Last updated 19 October 2026

Who we are

Operated by a UK sole trader trading as Plainsite. Contact: plainsite@agentmail.to. Postal address available on written request. Plainsite builds fast, fixed-price websites for UK sole traders and small businesses. For anything in this notice, email plainsite@agentmail.to. We are the “controller” (the organisation responsible) for the personal data described here.

What we collect

  • If you email us or use the contact form: your name, business name, email address and your message. The form only opens your own email app. The website itself does not store or send anything.
  • If you order a site: your contact details, what we need to write and build your site (services, area, opening hours), and the materials you send us: logo, photos and any customer reviews you choose to show.
  • Payments, once open, are handled by Stripe, which tells us your name, email, billing details, the last four digits of your card and whether payment succeeded. We never see your full card number.

Other people’s information in your materials

Photos and reviews you send us may include other people (for example a reviewer’s name). Please send only material you have the right to use. We use it only to build your site, on your instructions, acting on your behalf. Your own website will need its own privacy notice for your visitors. We can add one you supply, but we don’t write legal documents for you.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies. We count page visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service (goatcounter.com). It records the page, the referring site, browser screen size and an approximate country, but sets no cookies, does not store your IP address and does not track you across sites. Visits from browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are not counted. We use these counts only to see which pages are useful (legitimate interests) and keep them for no longer than 24 months. Fonts are served from our own site. Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Why we use your data (lawful basis)

  • Replying to enquiries: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • Building your site and providing any care plan: performance of our contract with you.
  • Taking payment and preventing fraud: contract, and our and Stripe’s legitimate interests.
  • Keeping business and tax records: legal obligation.

We never sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • AgentMail, which hosts our email inbox;
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers messages sent through our website contact form to our inbox;
  • Stripe, for payments (once payments open);
  • our website host (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.). Your own site is hosted wherever you choose, in your name.

AgentMail and GitHub are based in the United States, so personal data we share with them may be stored or processed there. GitHub handles our website host’s visitor logs under its own privacy statement. GitHub relies on the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (the ‘UK–US data bridge’) for transfers from the UK (see GitHub’s privacy statement). We do not have a separate data processing agreement with AgentMail, Inc., which hosts our email inbox in the United States, so emails sent to us are handled under AgentMail’s privacy notice. That notice says AgentMail may use the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers, and that, without such an agreement, it may use email content to improve and train its own services. Please don’t send us sensitive personal information by email. You can ask us for details at any time.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to an order: up to 12 months after our last contact. Order details, including your site files, materials and our working copies, are deleted 30 days after we deliver (launch); you keep your own copy. If you are on a care plan, we keep only what we need to run it and delete that 30 days after the plan ends. We keep only a basic order record (your name, email, date, what you bought and the amount) for as long as tax law requires (at least 5 years after the 31 January tax-return deadline for the year concerned).

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Email us to ask. Requests sent to our inbox are handled by the owner (not by automatic replies) within one month, as the law requires.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please tell us first by emailing plainsite@agentmail.to with “Data complaint” in the subject line. The owner will acknowledge it within 30 days, look into it without undue delay, keep you updated and tell you the outcome. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
